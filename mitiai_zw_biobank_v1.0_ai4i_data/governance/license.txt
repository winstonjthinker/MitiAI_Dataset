2. DATA — Sovereign Controlled Access Licence (custom)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The data is NOT open. This is deliberate, and it is the point.

An open data licence severs provenance at the first redistribution. Once a
record is copied without its consent conditions attached, the chain from a
compound back to the knowledge holder is broken and no benefit-sharing
claim can be proved. That severance is the mechanism by which extractive
bioprospecting works. This licence exists to prevent it.

TIERS

  Public
    Schema, data dictionary, metadata cards, validation code, aggregate
    statistics, and ward-level locations.
    Terms: open, attribution requested.

  Research
    Records classified public, raw sequence and spectra.
    Terms: Data Access Agreement with SIRDC. Institutional affiliation
    required. No re-identification attempt. No onward transfer. Findings
    citing this dataset must cite the originating community.

  Restricted
    Records classified community-restricted, field-precision coordinates,
    consented audio recordings.
    Terms: case by case. SIRDC approval AND concurrence of the relevant
    community council. Both are required; neither alone is sufficient.

  Commercial
    As negotiated.
    Terms: gated behind an executed benefit-sharing agreement with the
    originating community. No exceptions, and no retrospective agreements.

CONDITIONS BINDING ALL TIERS

  1. Community ownership. Communities retain ownership of their knowledge.
     SIRDC holds custody of the structured record. Custodial rights cannot
     be transferred to a commercial entity without explicit community
     consent.

  2. Attribution. Any publication drawing on a heritage record must credit
     the originating community. Where the holder has opted in
     (named_attribution_opt_in = 1), the holder is a named co-contributor.

  3. Withdrawal. A holder may withdraw at any time, unconditionally.
     Linked records are pseudonymised or deleted within 30 days. A
     recipient of any tier must action a withdrawal notice on the same
     terms and confirm it in writing.

  4. Onward licensing. This licence travels with the data. A recipient may
     not relicense under more permissive terms, including any open licence.

  5. Sacred knowledge. Not collected, not held, not licensable. There is no
     tier under which it could be released, because there is no field in
     the schema in which it could be stored.

  6. Prohibited uses, absolutely and at every tier: surveillance,
     individual identification, credit assessment, law enforcement, and
     profiling.

Governing law: Zimbabwe. Compliance with the Data Protection Act
[Chapter 12:07] and the Nagoya Protocol on Access and Benefit-sharing is a
condition of access at every tier.
