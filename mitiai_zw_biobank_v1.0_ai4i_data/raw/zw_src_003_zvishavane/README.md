# Source Register: ZW-SRC-003 — Zvishavane & Midlands Communal Woodland Sites

**Source ID**: ZW-SRC-003  
**Source Name**: Zvishavane Sites (Gokwe, Hwange, Midlands Communal Woodland)  
**Source Type**: Dryland Flora & Soil Microorganism Field Survey  
**Primary Steward**: SIRDC / Miti AI Consortium  
**Access Tier**: Public Field Collection Feed  

## 1. Context & Ecosystem Scope
This source directory contains primary field collection records from the Zvishavane, Gokwe, and Hwange communal woodland biomes. Sampling targets indigenous medicinal flora, drought-resilient rhizomes, and termite mound soils for secondary metabolite discovery.

## 2. Equipment & Field Methodology
- **GPS & Sensor Array**: WGS84 decimal degrees (EPSG:4326), precision-masked ward centroids, soil moisture sensors.
- **Sample Preservation**: Field silica-gel desiccation and cold-chain sample transport to SIRDC.

## 3. Governance & Legal Access
- **Permits**: District Environmental Officer permits and Ward Council endorsements (END-CC-MIDLANDS-*).
