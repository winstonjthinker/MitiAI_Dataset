# Source Register: ZW-SRC-004 — Traditional Heritage Knowledge Corpus

**Source ID**: ZW-SRC-004  
**Source Name**: Traditional Heritage Knowledge Holders & Healers  
**Source Type**: Ethnobotanical & Traditional Knowledge Survey  
**Primary Steward**: SIRDC / Miti AI Consortium  
**Access Tier**: Confidential / Controlled Access Feed  

## 1. Context & Ethical Safeguards
This source directory contains structured traditional knowledge survey records obtained through bilingual interviews (Shona and Ndebele) conducted by trained community liaisons. A 30% Ndebele language representation floor is enforced. Knowledge classified as sacred is strictly excluded from collection.

## 2. Anonymization & Cryptography
- **Holder Pseudonyms**: Named identities are replaced with pseudonyms (HLD-EH-*, HLD-LV-*, HLD-MW-*). Real identities reside exclusively in the AES-256 encrypted Holder Identity Register held in SIRDC custody.
- **Benefit-Sharing**: Every record links to a Benefit-Sharing Agreement (BSA-ZW-2026-*) guaranteeing royalty distribution upon commercial discovery.
