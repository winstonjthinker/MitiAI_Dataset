# Source Register: ZW-SRC-002 — Mazowe River Catchment & Lowveld Sites

**Source ID**: ZW-SRC-002  
**Source Name**: Mazowe River Catchment & Lowveld Communal Wards (Mazowe, Chiredzi, Chipinge)  
**Source Type**: Aquatic Environmental & Phytoremediation Field Survey  
**Primary Steward**: SIRDC / Miti AI Consortium  
**Access Tier**: Public Field Collection Feed  

## 1. Context & Ecological Scope
This source directory contains primary field collection feeds from aquatic, riverine sediment, and thermophilic soil habitats across the Mazowe River basin, Lake Chivero, and Chiredzi communal lowveld wards. Collection focuses on *Eichhornia crassipes* (water hyacinth) for phytoremediation, heavy metal bioaccumulation (Fe, Zn, Cr, Co), and 16S/ITS endophyte metagenomics.

## 2. Collection & Analytical Tools
- **Field Apparatus**: Water quality multi-parameter sondes (pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen), sediment corers.
- **Metagenomics & Metabolomics**: Nanopore field metagenomics and LC-MS extracts of aquatic tissues.

## 3. Governance & Access Status
- **Permit Reference**: ZimParks Research Access & District Environmental Officer permits.
- **Community Endorsement**: Ward Community Council endorsements (END-CC-MAZOWE-*).
