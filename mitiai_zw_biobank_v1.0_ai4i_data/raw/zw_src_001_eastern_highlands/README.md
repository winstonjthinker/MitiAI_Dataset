# Source Register: ZW-SRC-001 — Eastern Highlands Field Collection Sites

**Source ID**: ZW-SRC-001  
**Source Name**: Eastern Highlands Field Sites (Nyanga, Mutare, Chimanimani Wards)  
**Source Type**: Field Specimen & Environmental Survey  
**Primary Steward**: SIRDC / Miti AI Consortium  
**Access Tier**: Public Field Collection Feed  

## 1. Context & Geographic Scope
This source directory contains primary field collection logs and environmental sensor feeds captured across montane forest, riverine, and communal agricultural wards in Manicaland (Nyanga, Mutare, and Chimanimani). The target flora focus on medicinal ginger lineages (*Siphonochilus aethiopicus* / *Zingiber officinale*) and pepper-bark tree specimens (*Warburgia salutaris*).

## 2. Collection Tools & Analytical Equipment
- **Genomic Barcoding**: Mobile Oxford Nanopore MinION sequencing (SQK-RBK114-24 kit, R10.4.1 flowcells).
- **Environmental Sensing**: WGS84 GPS (ward-centroid precision masked), soil pH meters, temperature sensors, canopy cover meters.
- **Metabolomic Extraction**: Silica-gel field preservation and methanol:water extracts for SIRDC LC-MS core processing.

## 3. Governance & Legal Access
- **Permit Reference**: NBA Bioprospecting Permit (Phase I) under National Biotechnology Authority authorisation.
- **Community Endorsement**: Local Rural District Council and Ward Community Council endorsements (END-CC-MANICALAND-*).
- **Prior Informed Consent**: Prior Informed Consent (FPIC) logged before physical collection.
