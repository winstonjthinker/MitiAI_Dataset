# Source Register: ZW-SRC-005 — National Herbarium of Zimbabwe

**Source ID**: ZW-SRC-005  
**Source Name**: National Herbarium of Zimbabwe Accessions & Vouchers  
**Source Type**: Institutional Voucher Catalog & Digitized Accessions  
**Primary Steward**: National Herbarium / SIRDC  
**Access Tier**: Public Institutional Catalog  

## 1. Context & Taxonomy Mapping
This source directory contains digitized herbarium voucher accession records, botanical determinations, and taxonomic cross-references linking physical voucher specimens (SRGH-*) to molecular barcoding markers (*rbcL*, *matK*, *ITS*).

## 2. Institutional Agreements
- **MOU**: Institutional Memorandum of Understanding between SIRDC and the National Herbarium of Zimbabwe.
